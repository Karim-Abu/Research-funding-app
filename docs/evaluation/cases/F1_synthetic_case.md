## 7. SOL.INNOVATION_SOLUTION.EDITOR

**Expected status:** present but weak / reviewer risk  
**Synthetic content:**

### State of the art

Current industrial monitoring systems use rule-based thresholds, statistical process control and predictive-maintenance models. These approaches can detect anomalies, but they often provide limited explanation of why a quality deviation occurs and how it relates to process parameters.

Recent AI approaches in manufacturing use machine learning for anomaly detection and process optimization. However, many solutions require extensive customization and do not provide sufficient explanation for quality engineers.

### Novelty of the solution

QUALIMON combines machine signals, quality measurements and process context in an explainable AI module. The system should identify early deviation patterns and propose possible causes. The project aims to move from pure anomaly detection to actionable quality-deviation support.

The expected technical goals are:

- 20 percent reduction in scrap
- 30 percent faster detection of critical deviations
- at least 85 percent precision in detecting relevant deviation events

The baseline, measurement method and validation time point are not yet fully defined.

### Preliminary work

The implementation partner has access to production monitoring data and quality reports from pilot lines. The research partner has experience with explainable AI and industrial analytics. The exact data quality, annotation process and legal basis for using the production data still need to be clarified.

### Partner suitability

The implementation partner provides pilot access, domain knowledge and industrial validation. The research partner provides AI methodology and evaluation expertise. Evidence for partner track record and infrastructure should still be linked.

No patent search or Freedom-to-Operate analysis has been completed yet. The project team assumes that the main innovation will be protected as software know-how and trade secret, but the ownership of newly developed models, interfaces and datasets still needs to be clarified between the partners.
